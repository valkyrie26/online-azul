    How to play

    1. Draft. On your turn, pick one colour from a factory (you take all tiles of that colour there; the rest slide to the centre) or from the centre pile.
    2. Place. Put the tiles on one pattern line on the left of your board. A line holds a single colour and must not already have that colour on the wall in the same row. Tiles that do not fit fall to the floor line.
    3. First player. The first person to take from the centre also takes the 1 marker: it costs a floor spot but you start the next round.
    4. Wall tiling. When every factory and the centre are empty, each complete pattern line moves its rightmost tile to the matching wall slot. Score 1 point, or the length of the horizontal and vertical runs it joins. Leftover tiles go to the lid.
    5. Floor. Floor tiles cost −1 −1 −2 −2 −2 −3 −3. Your score never drops below zero.
    6. Game end. After the round in which someone completes a horizontal wall row. Bonuses: +2 per complete row, +7 per complete column, +10 per colour with all five tiles placed.

    Controls: click tiles to pick a colour, then click a pattern line or the floor. Drag to orbit the table, scroll to zoom. Use the eye buttons to inspect opponents' boards.
